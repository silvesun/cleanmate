Frequently asked questions

I cleaned, so why didn't free space go up?

Cleaned files are in the Trash and still take up space. When you're sure you don't need them, empty the Trash in Finder. CleanMate never empties the Trash for you.

I removed something by mistake. What now?

Click Undo in the banner at the bottom of the window, or Rollback in Cleanup History, and the files return to where they were. You can also open the Trash, Control-click a file and choose Put Back. As long as the Trash hasn't been emptied, you can recover it.

Is it harmful to delete caches?

No. Caches are temporary data that apps rebuild when needed. Some apps may open a little slower the first time afterwards.

Why does CleanMate ask me to choose a folder?

CleanMate follows macOS security rules and can only open folders you grant. Choose your home folder the first time you scan, and the Applications folder when you uninstall an app. You can check access at any time on the app's Security & Permissions page.

Why are some items missing or not selected?

CleanMate only preselects items it knows are safe. Items that may hold your data, such as device backups or downloaded installers, are yours to confirm; caches of running apps are skipped; and keychains, iCloud Drive, your Photos library and other personal data are never offered for cleaning.

It says the app is running when I uninstall.

To prevent data loss, CleanMate won't uninstall an app that is running. Quit the app (⌘Q) first, then uninstall it.

System requirements

  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • Mac with Apple silicon or an Intel processor
