Clean up safely. Undo anytime.

SafeSweep finds files on your Mac that are safe to remove and frees up disk space. Everything goes to the Trash first, so one click puts it all back.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store · Requires macOS 14 or later
✨

One-click Smart Clean

Scans only app caches and logs, which apps rebuild on their own. One button to scan, one to clean.

🛡️

Personal data stays untouched

Keychains, iCloud Drive, your Photos library, Mail and Messages are never cleaned. Apps that are running are skipped.

↩️

Undo in one click

An Undo button appears after every cleanup, and Cleanup History can restore files later. SafeSweep never empties your Trash.

🧰

More tools

App uninstaller and leftovers, large and long-unopened files, duplicates, and Xcode and developer caches.

🔒 No internet. No data collection. Every scan runs on your Mac. No accounts, ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
