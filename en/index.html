Clean up safely. Undo anything.

SafeSweep finds files on your Mac that are safe to remove and frees up disk space. Everything goes to the Trash first, so one click puts it all back.

Coming soon to the Mac App Store · Requires macOS 14 or later
✨

One-click Smart Clean

Finds the caches and logs that apps rebuild on their own, and points out large files, developer caches, leftovers of removed apps and old installers for you to review.

🛡️

Your data stays yours

Items that may hold your data are never pre-selected, and anything changed after the scan is left alone. Keychains, iCloud Drive, Mail and Messages are never cleaned. Running apps are skipped.

↩️

Undo anything

Every cleanup can be undone, and Cleanup History restores files for as long as they are in the Trash. SafeSweep never empties your Trash.

🧰

More tools

Disk Map, app uninstaller and leftovers, duplicate files and similar photos, Xcode and developer caches, and a menu bar monitor.

🔒 No internet. No data collection. Every scan runs on your Mac. No accounts, ads or analytics. See the Privacy Policy.
